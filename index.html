About Me

Hello! I am Zoe Stephens, a rising senior student-athlete at Wellesley College majoring in Cognitive Science with a concentration in Computer Science. When I'm not studying, I enjoy spending time baking, reading, and lifting weights.

I am interested in sociolinguistics, particularly when looking at language used for elitism. I have previously done research on how text-formatting impacts personae formation, and am currently working in a Biology lab researching behavioral patterns of the Southern Giant Hornet (Vespa soror). I am interested in a wide range of subject matter—including biology, computer science, and linguistics—as well as how these fields interact.

I believe strongly in passion and curiosity as the driving factors of innovation, and I am always looking for ways to be more involved in the STEM research community. Reach out to connect!
